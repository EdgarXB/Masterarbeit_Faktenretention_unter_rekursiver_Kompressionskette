# Human Validation Review

Thank you for helping with this review.

## What To Do

1. Open `human_validation_review_app.html` in your browser.
2. Review each item one at a time.
3. Compare the **question**, **summary text**, and **generated answer**.
4. Choose one **Question Quality** label.
5. Choose one **Answer Label**.
6. Add notes only when helpful.
7. Click **Export answers** when finished.
8. Send back the downloaded `human_validation_answers.csv`.

Your progress is saved automatically in the browser on the same computer.

## Files

- `human_validation_review_app.html`: the review app.
- `APP_REVIEWER_INSTRUCTIONS.md`: detailed instructions.
- `EMAIL_TEMPLATE.md`: the message that explained the task.

## Time Estimate

There are 60 review items shown in randomized order. The task usually takes about 45-75 minutes.

If you cannot finish all items, please still click **Export answers** and send back the partial result.
